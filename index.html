The real reason to visit

Your South Coast getaway starts here.

Driftsands 108 is your base. The experience is everything around it — beach mornings, lagoon adventures, fishing, neighbouring seaside towns and memorable day trips.

🌊

Uvongo Beach

A distinctive coastal setting with beach, rock pools, tidal pool, pier and the famous waterfall-and-lagoon backdrop.

💦

Waterfall & Lagoon

Uvongo's 23-metre waterfall drops into the lagoon behind the beach — one of the area's signature sights.

🛶

Paddle & Explore

Kayaks and pedal boats are available around the lagoon area, offering another way to enjoy the scenery. Always follow current local water-safety signage.

🎣

Fishing

Try your luck from the local pier and rocks, or make a day of it with a South Coast deep-sea fishing charter.

🐋

Whale Season

During the seasonal migration, watch the ocean from the balcony and along the South Coast for the chance to spot passing whales. Sightings are seasonal and not guaranteed.

🏖️

Beach Hop

Explore Uvongo, Manaba, Margate, Ramsgate, Shelly Beach and other coastal spots without being tied to just one beach.

🛍️

Easy & Convenient

Groceries, restaurants and everyday essentials are nearby, with Margate and Shelly Beach also within easy reach.

Go beyond the beach

Turn a few nights into an adventure.

Oribi Gorge & Wild 5 Adventures

Swap the coastline for dramatic gorge scenery and adventure activities. Wild 5 operates in Oribi Gorge and offers experiences including quad expeditions and gorge-based adventures.

Explore Wild 5 →

Lake Eland & the South Coast interior

Plan a scenic day away from the shoreline and discover the landscapes around the Oribi Gorge area. This is the kind of outing that makes Driftsands 108 more than a beach-only holiday base.

Deep-sea fishing

For anglers, the coast offers opportunities to turn a holiday morning into a full offshore fishing experience. Charter availability and sea conditions should always be confirmed directly with operators.

Make your own South Coast route

Mix beach days with local restaurants, shopping, coastal walks, fishing and inland adventures. Stay central, then choose a different experience each day.
